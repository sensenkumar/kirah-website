Frequently Asked Questions

Can I subscribe to more than one product?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise plans support running multiple Kirah products together, with shared billing and a single support relationship.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

No separate setup fee. Onboarding, data migration, and initial training are included in every plan, with faster turnaround on higher tiers.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, switching to yearly billing saves roughly 20% compared to paying monthly, on every plan.

Can I switch plans or products later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or add another Kirah product at any time without losing your existing data.

Is staff training included?

Every plan includes initial staff training. Professional and Enterprise plans include ongoing training sessions as your team grows.
