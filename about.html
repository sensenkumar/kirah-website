OUR STORY

One Team, Three Industries We Understand

Kirah was founded to prove that software can be both deeply specialized and beautifully simple — one product at a time.

HOW WE STARTED

From One Problem to Three Platforms

Kirah began when our founders — a lawyer, a hotelier, and a hospital administrator — kept running into the same issue: the software available to them was either too generic or too expensive to justify. Each built the tool they wished existed. Together, those tools became Kirah.

Today, BenchMark, Room2Service, and DocApp run independently but share the same engineering team, support standards, and obsession with getting the small details right.

2018 — Founded

Kirah is founded by three industry operators frustrated with generic software.

2019 — BenchMark Launches

Our first product ships for independent legal practices and court staff.

2021 — Room2Service Joins

A unified POS & PMS platform launches for boutique hotels and resorts.

2023 — DocApp Completes the Suite

A full reception, patient, and pharmacy suite ships for clinics and hospitals.

2026 — Today

700+ clients across three industries, one shared commitment to craft.
